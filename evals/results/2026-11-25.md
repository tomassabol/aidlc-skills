## Findings, all fixed in the skills
1. **Bugfix friction.** A one-line bugfix went through 2 question rounds (practices, then requirements) and was asked 3 irrelevant extension opt-ins. Fix: put the practices questions into the Requirements file, skip the extension opt-ins for bugfix and trivial profiles, and aim for one round of 5 questions or fewer.
2. **Reviewer findings resolved by invention.** In 02, the agent "fixed" 3 major review findings by adding new product rules as assumptions. Fix (`reviewer.md`): the agent fixes mechanical findings itself. Product or policy findings become questions, or are listed as "Decided by me, please confirm".
3. **Scope bloat for a tiny team.** 30 FRs and 21 assumptions for a 1 to 2 person, under-a-month project triggered no rule. Fix: new conflict rule RQ-8 proposes an MVP cut.
4. **Resume at an open gate.** The good behaviour seen in 04 (re-presenting the gate rather than asking A/B/X) is now written into the resume rules.
